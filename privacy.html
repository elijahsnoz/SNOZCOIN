1. Introduction

SNOZCOIN ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the SNOZCOIN platform.

Key Privacy Principle: SNOZCOIN is a non-custodial platform. We never have access to your private keys, and we do not store your cryptocurrency. Your wallet and assets remain under your complete control.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

Data Type Purpose Storage
Wallet Address Platform identification, transaction linking Local storage only
Creator Profile (optional) Display name, bio, profile image On-chain or IPFS
Content Metadata Content titles, descriptions, pricing On-chain
Contact Email (optional) Support communications Encrypted database

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

Data Type Purpose Collection Method
Device Information Compatibility, debugging Browser APIs
IP Address Security, fraud prevention Server logs (anonymized)
Usage Data Platform improvement Analytics (optional)
Wallet Connection Status UI state management Session storage

2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Private Keys: We never ask for or have access to your private keys
  • Recovery Phrases: We never request your seed phrase or mnemonic
  • Financial Account Info: No bank accounts, credit cards, or traditional financial data
  • Government IDs: We do not collect identity documents (no KYC required)

3. Blockchain Data

Important: The Stacks blockchain is a public, transparent, and immutable ledger. All transactions you make through SNOZCOIN are recorded on-chain and are publicly visible.

3.1 Public Blockchain Information

The following information is recorded on the public blockchain and cannot be modified or deleted:

  • Your wallet address
  • All transaction history (tips, content unlocks, SNOZ transfers)
  • Smart contract interactions
  • On-chain content metadata
  • SNOZ token balances and tier status

3.2 Pseudonymity

While blockchain data is public, your wallet address is pseudonymous. Your real-world identity is not directly linked to your wallet address unless you choose to disclose it (e.g., in your creator profile).

4. How We Use Information

We use collected information for the following purposes:

  • Platform Operation: Enabling core features like tipping, content access, and rewards
  • User Interface: Displaying your wallet connection status, balances, and tier
  • Security: Detecting and preventing fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access
  • Improvement: Analyzing usage patterns to improve the platform
  • Communication: Sending important platform updates (if you provide email)
  • Legal Compliance: Complying with applicable laws and regulations

5. Data Sharing

5.1 When We Share Data

  • With Your Consent: When you explicitly authorize sharing
  • Service Providers: Third parties who help operate our platform (hosting, analytics)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or government request
  • Safety: To protect the rights, property, or safety of users or others

5.2 We Do NOT Sell Your Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

6. Data Retention

Data Type Retention Period

Server Logs 30 days (anonymized)

Blockchain Data Permanent (immutable)

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data we hold
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data (except blockchain data)
  • Portability: Receive your data in a portable format
  • Objection: Object to certain processing activities
  • Restriction: Request limitation of processing

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@snozcoin.xyz.

Note: We cannot delete or modify data stored on the blockchain. Blockchain transactions are permanent and immutable by design.

8. Cookies & Analytics

8.1 Local Storage

We use browser local storage to:

  • Remember your wallet connection preference
  • Store UI preferences (theme, onboarding status)
  • Cache certain data for performance

8.2 Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics to understand how users interact with the platform. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or advertising cookies.

9. Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information:

  • HTTPS encryption for all connections
  • Regular security audits of our codebase
  • Open-source smart contracts for transparency
  • No storage of sensitive credentials
  • Rate limiting and DDoS protection

However, no system is 100% secure. You are responsible for securing your own wallet and devices.

10. Children's Privacy

SNOZCOIN is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 18, we will delete that information promptly.

11. International Users

SNOZCOIN operates globally. If you access the platform from outside our primary jurisdiction, you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in other countries where data protection laws may differ from your jurisdiction.

By using SNOZCOIN, you consent to the transfer of your information as described in this policy.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by:

  • Posting the updated policy on this page
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date
  • Providing prominent notice on the platform

Your continued use of SNOZCOIN after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

13. Contact Us
