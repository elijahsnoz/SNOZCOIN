1. Abstract

SNOZCOIN is a decentralized creator monetization platform built on Stacks, a Bitcoin Layer 2 network. The platform enables creators to receive direct payments, offer exclusive content, build subscription-based communities, and reward their most loyal supporters—all secured by Bitcoin's finality.

Unlike traditional creator platforms that extract 30-50% fees and control creator relationships, SNOZCOIN puts creators in control. Payments flow directly to creators in STX (Stacks tokens), while SNOZ serves as a non-speculative utility token for reputation, governance, and platform rewards.

Key Innovation: SNOZCOIN separates monetary value (STX) from platform utility (SNOZ), eliminating speculation on utility tokens while still providing meaningful engagement incentives.

To provide creators with transparent, non-custodial tools for monetization, community building, and fan engagement—powered by Bitcoin security and smart contract automation.

3. Problem Statement

The creator economy is broken. Despite generating over $250 billion annually, creators face systemic challenges:

Platform Dependency

  • Creators don't own their audience relationships
  • Platforms can demonetize, deplatform, or change algorithms without recourse
  • Content and follower data are siloed and non-portable

Unfair Revenue Splits

  • Traditional platforms take 30-50% of creator earnings
  • Payment processing adds additional fees
  • International creators face currency conversion costs

Lack of Direct Monetization

  • Tips go through intermediaries with delays and fees
  • Subscription payments are controlled by platforms
  • No transparent way to reward loyal fans

Web3 Complexity

  • Existing blockchain solutions are too complex for mainstream creators
  • High gas fees make micropayments impractical
  • Security risks from smart contract vulnerabilities

5. Technical Architecture

Why Stacks (Bitcoin L2)?

Stacks is the leading Bitcoin Layer 2 for smart contracts. It provides:

  • Bitcoin Finality: All Stacks transactions settle on Bitcoin
  • Clarity Language: Decidable, non-Turing complete smart contracts that prevent entire classes of bugs
  • sBTC Integration: Native Bitcoin programmability (coming soon)
  • Low Fees: Transaction costs suitable for micropayments
  • Proof of Transfer: Mining secured by Bitcoin's hash power

Stack Overview

  • Smart Contracts: Clarity 3 on Stacks (Epoch 3.1)
  • Frontend: Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS with Stacks Connect SDK
  • Wallets: Leather and Xverse (Stacks), Phantom and Solflare (Solana)
  • APIs: Hiro API for blockchain data, Jupiter API for swaps
  • Testing: Clarinet with Vitest (223 unit tests)

9. Governance

SNOZCOIN operates as a decentralized autonomous organization (DAO) where token holders shape platform development:

Proposal Types

  • Feature Proposals: New platform functionality
  • Parameter Changes: Adjust reward rates, tier thresholds
  • Treasury Allocation: Fund grants, partnerships, development
  • Protocol Upgrades: Smart contract improvements

Voting Process

  1. Any Gold-tier (10,000+ SNOZ) member can submit a proposal
  2. 7-day discussion period for community feedback
  3. 7-day voting period with SNOZ-weighted votes
  4. Proposals require >50% approval and minimum 10% quorum
  5. Approved proposals are executed by the DAO contract

Treasury

The SNOZCOIN treasury is controlled by the DAO and funds:

  • Platform development and maintenance
  • Security audits and bug bounties
  • Creator grants and onboarding incentives
  • Community events and marketing
