3. Platform Description

SNOZCOIN is a decentralized creator monetization platform built on the Stacks blockchain, secured by Bitcoin. The Platform enables:

  • Direct Creator Support: Users can send STX (Stacks tokens) directly to creators as tips
  • Content Access: Creators can offer gated content that users unlock with STX payments
  • Reward System: Users earn SNOZ tokens (non-monetary utility tokens) for platform participation
  • Community Features: Tier-based access levels and community governance participation

The Platform operates through smart contracts deployed on the Stacks blockchain. All transactions are public, transparent, and immutable once confirmed on-chain.
