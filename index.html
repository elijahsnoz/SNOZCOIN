Frequently Asked Questions

Honest answers about SNOZCOIN.

What is SNOZCOIN?

SNOZCOIN is a scalable creator monetization platform built on Stacks, leveraging Bitcoin's security. Creators receive direct STX payments for tips and content, while earning SNOZ loyalty rewards.

What is STX vs SNOZ?

STX is the currency — used for all payments, tips, and content purchases. SNOZ is a non-tradeable utility token for rewards, reputation, and governance. You can't buy or sell SNOZ.

How do creators earn money?

Creators receive STX directly from fans through tipping and content unlocks. 100% of payments go to creators (minus minimal network fees). No middlemen, no platform cuts.

How do I earn SNOZ?

Earn SNOZ by tipping creators (2 SNOZ per STX), buying content (3 SNOZ per STX), or registering as a creator (200 SNOZ bonus). Higher SNOZ balances unlock better tiers and perks.

Why Stacks and Bitcoin?

Stacks brings smart contracts to Bitcoin. All SNOZCOIN transactions inherit Bitcoin's security through Proof of Transfer. Your funds are protected by the most secure blockchain.

What wallets work with SNOZCOIN?

Connect with Leather or Xverse wallets. Both support Stacks and make it easy to tip creators, buy content, and check your SNOZ balance.

Is this a speculative investment?

No. SNOZCOIN is a creator platform, not an investment. SNOZ tokens have no monetary value and cannot be traded. STX is the only currency used for payments.
